# Product Ideas — Axle to $1M ARR

1. **Instant VIN-scan valuation** — point your phone camera at a VIN plate, decode it, and get a comp-based price range in under 5 seconds. Killer top-of-funnel hook.
2. **Price-drop alerts via SMS/push** — watchlists that text you the moment a matching vehicle dips below your target price. Freemium with paid unlimited alerts.
3. **Seller pricing copilot** — sellers paste their Craigslist/FB link, Axle tells them if they're over/under-priced vs. live comps and suggests an optimal ask.
4. **Embedded valuation widget (API)** — licensable JS snippet + REST API that forums, dealerships, and indie apps embed for $0.05–$0.25/lookup.
5. **Pre-purchase inspection marketplace** — connect buyers with mobile mechanics; Axle takes a referral cut and folds inspection findings into the valuation.
6. **Offer negotiation assistant** — AI-guided counter-offer suggestions grounded in comp data, increasing deal close rate and average transaction value.
7. **Market trend reports (paid tier)** — weekly/monthly segment reports (e.g., "E46 M3 market Q2 2026") with depreciation curves, days-on-market, and regional heat maps.
8. **Saved search syndication** — one watchlist fans out across Craigslist, FB Marketplace, BaT, C&B, and AutoTempest; users pay to add more sources.
9. **Escrow-lite / secure payment rails** — integrate Stripe Connect or Plaid for buyer-seller fund holds, earning float income + transaction fees.
10. **Title & lien verification add-on** — one-click NMVTIS check bundled at checkout; high margin, high trust signal.
11. **Dealer-to-private arbitrage feed** — flag vehicles priced significantly below private-party value at dealer lots; premium subscription for flippers.
12. **Modification value calculator** — quantify how specific mods (coilovers, turbo kit, wheels) affect resale using comp deltas; upsell to enthusiast audience.
13. **Regional pricing intelligence** — show where the same vehicle is cheapest/most expensive by ZIP, enabling long-distance deal hunting for power buyers.
14. **Seasonal pricing optimizer** — tell sellers the best month to list (convertibles in spring, 4x4s in fall) backed by historical comp seasonality curves.
15. **White-label valuation for credit unions & lenders** — banks need private-party collateral values for auto loans; high-contract-value B2B channel.
